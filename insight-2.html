Perspective / DIGITAL LEARNING

Designing learning around the people who use it

Effective digital learning begins with real needs and clear, accessible experiences.

Begin with the learner’s context: their goals, available time and existing knowledge. Organise content around clear outcomes, offer opportunities to practise and make navigation accessible. Feedback from learners can help guide thoughtful improvements.
